# Employee handbook

## Working hours

Regular working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Employees may
request flexible hours with the approval of their team lead.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. A stable internet connection and
availability during core hours (10:00 AM to 3:00 PM) are required.

## Code of conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.
Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated.
